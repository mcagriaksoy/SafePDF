Key features

OFF

Offline Processing

All PDF operations run entirely on your local device without requiring any internet connection, ensuring your sensitive files never leave your computer or get uploaded to external servers.

PRV

Privacy‑Focused

This privacy-first toolkit implements minimal telemetry practices and provides clear, transparent consent controls for managing optional cookies, giving you complete control over your data.

OSS

Open Source

The complete source code for SafePDF is publicly available on GitHub under the GPL‑3.0 license, allowing anyone to review, audit, and contribute to the project's development.

CMP

Smart Compression

Our intelligent compression engine offers multiple quality presets and advanced image controls, allowing you to find the perfect balance between reduced file size and maintained visual quality.

SPL

Split & Extract

Easily split large PDF documents by individual pages, specific page ranges, or extract single pages quickly for sharing or further processing as separate files.

MRG

Merge PDFs

Combine multiple PDF documents into a single unified file with the ability to reorder pages and arrange content exactly how you need it before exporting the final document.

IMG

Export Images

Export individual PDF pages as high‑quality AVIF or JPG images, perfect for creating previews, thumbnails, or preparing content for further image processing and editing.

TXT

Text Conversion

Convert PDF documents to searchable plain text format or editable DOCX files, making it easy to reuse content, perform text analysis, or continue editing in word processors.

ROT

Rotate Pages

Rotate individual pages or entire documents non‑destructively, preserving the original content quality while fixing orientation issues in scanned or incorrectly formatted PDF files.

RPR

Repair PDFs

Attempt to recover and repair corrupted or damaged PDF files by salvaging readable pages and reconstructing document structure to retrieve as much content as possible.

MET

Metadata Editing

Quickly edit and update PDF metadata including document title, author information, subject fields, keywords, and other properties to better organize and identify your files.

CLI

CLI & Automation

Automate repetitive PDF tasks using command-line interface examples and scripts, perfect for batch processing large numbers of documents or integrating into existing workflows.
